Monsoon Rice Yield Study 2024

This is a fictional research summary about rice yields in the fictional district of Chandrapur during the 2024 monsoon season. The study was conducted by the Chandrapur Agricultural Institute.

Findings: rice yields in Chandrapur averaged 4.2 tonnes per hectare in 2024, up from 3.6 tonnes per hectare in 2023. The increase is attributed to the new Sona-9 rice variety and to rainfall that was 18 percent above the ten-year average.

Irrigation: fields in Chandrapur that used drip irrigation produced 5.1 tonnes per hectare, compared with 3.9 tonnes per hectare for flood-irrigated fields. Drip irrigation also reduced water use by 31 percent per hectare over the season.

Pests: the brown planthopper affected 14 percent of surveyed fields in Chandrapur in 2024. Fields treated with neem-based spray within 10 days of first sighting lost only 2 percent of yield, while untreated fields lost 19 percent.

Recommendation: the Chandrapur Agricultural Institute recommends planting Sona-9 by 15 June and applying the first fertiliser dose 21 days after transplanting. The institute will run a follow-up trial on 40 farms in the 2025 season.
