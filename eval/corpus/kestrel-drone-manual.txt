Kestrel Drone Model K-7 User Manual

The Kestrel K-7 is a fictional quadcopter drone built for agricultural surveying. This manual covers battery, range, camera and safety rules for the K-7 only.

Battery: the Kestrel K-7 uses a 5200 mAh lithium battery that provides up to 38 minutes of flight time in calm wind. The battery must be charged with the official K-7 charger, and charging takes approximately 75 minutes from empty to full.

Range: the Kestrel K-7 has a maximum control range of 9 kilometres in open terrain with no interference. If the drone loses signal for more than 10 seconds, it automatically returns to its takeoff point at an altitude of 60 metres.

Camera: the Kestrel K-7 carries a 20 megapixel camera with a 3-axis gimbal stabiliser. It records 4K video at 30 frames per second and stores footage on a microSD card of up to 256 gigabytes.

Safety: the Kestrel K-7 must not be flown above 120 metres altitude or within 5 kilometres of an airport. Flights are prohibited in rain, and the propellers must be inspected for cracks before every flight.
