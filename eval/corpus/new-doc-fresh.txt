Cedar Bakery Supplier Terms

Cedar Bakery is a fictional bakery in Nashik. This document describes the supplier payment terms that apply to all flour, sugar and dairy suppliers of Cedar Bakery.

Payment: Cedar Bakery pays suppliers within 21 days of invoice receipt. Invoices must list the delivery date, the quantity in kilograms, and the batch number. Late payments incur interest of 1.5 percent per month on the outstanding amount.

Delivery: flour deliveries to Cedar Bakery arrive every Tuesday and Friday before 7 am. If a delivery is more than 2 hours late, the bakery may refuse it and order from the backup supplier in Sinnar instead.

Quality: all flour supplied to Cedar Bakery must have a protein content of at least 11.5 percent. Each delivery is tested on arrival, and a failed test means the whole batch is returned at the supplier's expense.

Orders: Cedar Bakery places orders by email before 4 pm on the day before delivery. The minimum order is 50 kilograms of flour, and orders above 500 kilograms receive a 4 percent bulk discount.
