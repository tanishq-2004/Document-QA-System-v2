Aurora Solar Employee Leave Policy

Aurora Solar is a fictional solar panel installation company based in Jaipur. This document describes the leave policy that applies to all full-time installation technicians and office staff.

Annual leave: every full-time employee at Aurora Solar receives 24 days of paid annual leave per year. Annual leave must be requested at least 14 days in advance through the Aurora Solar staff portal, and no more than 10 consecutive days may be taken during the monsoon installation season.

Sick leave: Aurora Solar grants 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required only when sick leave exceeds 3 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry forward to the next year at Aurora Solar.

Remote work: office staff at Aurora Solar may work remotely up to 2 days per week, but installation technicians must be on site. Remote work requests are approved by the team lead and recorded in the weekly roster.

Reimbursement: Aurora Solar reimburses travel expenses up to 800 rupees per day for technicians visiting customer sites outside Jaipur city limits. Fuel receipts must be submitted within 7 days of the trip, and reimbursement is paid with the next monthly salary.
