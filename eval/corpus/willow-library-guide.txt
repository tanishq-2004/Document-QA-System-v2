Willow Public Library Membership Guide

Willow Public Library is a fictional public library in Indore. This guide explains membership, borrowing rules, fines and opening hours for the Willow Public Library.

Membership: annual membership at Willow Public Library costs 450 rupees for adults and 150 rupees for students under 18. Membership includes access to all three Willow branches and the digital e-book collection of 12,000 titles.

Borrowing: members of Willow Public Library may borrow up to 6 books at a time for 21 days. Books may be renewed twice, each renewal adding 14 days, unless another member has reserved the book.

Fines: Willow Public Library charges a late fine of 5 rupees per book per day, capped at 200 rupees per book. Lost books are charged at the replacement cost plus a 100 rupee processing fee.

Hours: Willow Public Library is open Monday to Saturday from 9 am to 8 pm, and on Sundays from 10 am to 2 pm. The library is closed on national holidays and on the second Tuesday of every month for stock maintenance.
