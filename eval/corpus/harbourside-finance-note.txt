Harbourside Cafe Quarterly Finance Note Q2 2026

Harbourside Cafe is a fictional cafe in Kochi. This note summarises its finances for the second quarter of 2026, covering April, May and June.

Revenue: Harbourside Cafe earned total revenue of 18.4 lakh rupees in Q2 2026. June was the strongest month at 7.1 lakh rupees, driven by weekend brunch service, while April was the weakest at 5.2 lakh rupees.

Costs: the largest cost at Harbourside Cafe in Q2 2026 was staff wages at 6.3 lakh rupees, followed by ingredients at 4.8 lakh rupees. Rent for the Kochi waterfront premises was fixed at 1.9 lakh rupees per month throughout the quarter.

Profit: Harbourside Cafe recorded a net profit of 2.6 lakh rupees in Q2 2026, a profit margin of 14 percent. The owners plan to reinvest 1.5 lakh rupees of the profit in a new espresso machine and outdoor seating.

Outlook: Harbourside Cafe expects Q3 2026 revenue to grow by 9 percent, helped by the Onam festival season. The cafe will hire 3 additional part-time staff for the festival period, starting 20 August 2026.
